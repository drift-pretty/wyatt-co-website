OUR STORY

Created from lived experience.

Ally Richardson — Founder

Wyatt & Co Innovation Studio was created from real life lived experiences and a desire to improve our own family’s safety, organisation and everyday life while helping other families do the same.

The studio brings together family support, accessibility, travel, lifestyle, home and automotive ideas under the Compass ecosystem.

Every concept begins with a genuine need and is shaped around practical, human-centred use.
